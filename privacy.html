Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version

Jankro Run has no accounts and we do not collect, see or sell any of your information. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows adverts from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one, but Google's advertising service runs in the game from when it opens and receives some information from your device, as described below.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: your best scores, the patties you have collected, the items you hold, the checkpoints you have reached, and your sound and game-mode settings. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Adverts (Google AdMob)

After a crash you can choose to watch an advert to carry on, or to double the patties from your run. These adverts are provided by Google AdMob. So that an advert is ready when you want one, the game starts Google's advertising service when it opens and loads an advert in the background.

When it does this, Google's service collects some information from your device, such as your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), device type and settings, app and advert interactions, and diagnostic data, and uses it to deliver, measure and improve adverts and to prevent fraud. Google is responsible for that information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The game does not ask your permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not give Google your device's advertising identifier. You can also turn off personalised adverts from Apple in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

Purchases

There are no purchases with real money in the game. The shop uses patties collected while playing.

Children

Jankro Run is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. We do not collect any personal information ourselves.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
